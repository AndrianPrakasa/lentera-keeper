Keep the night market lit. Shadows swarm in waves — blast them with lantern light, gather star shards, and choose a blessing between waves. Survive all 10 waves and fell the Raksasa Bayang to win. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best game” bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no dependencies, no network: plain static files under `game/`, all art drawn on canvas and all sound synthesised with WebAudio.

## How to play

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys
- **Aim:** mouse
- **Shoot:** hold click or Space
- **Blessings:** click a card or press 1 / 2 / 3 between waves
- **Pause:** P · **Mute:** M · **Restart:** Space or click on the end screen

Three hearts, ten waves. Moths are fast and weak, wisps zigzag, beetles are tough, and the Raksasa Bayang brings friends on waves 5 and 10. Kills build a combo multiplier; shards and power-ups (heart, rapid fire, shield) drop from fallen shadows.
